Data Deletion Instructions

Spectre Assistant is a private tool for a single Threads account. It does not have user accounts. The only personal data it may hold about other people is the text, username and link of a public Threads post that matched a search such as "need a website".

To have your data deleted

  1. Send a message on Telegram to @hovatsavsem, or a direct message to @ilya.kelnovich on Threads.
  2. Tell us your Threads username (for example, "delete everything about @yourname").
  3. We will delete all records connected to that username and confirm within 7 days.

Automatic deletion

Saved public posts are deleted automatically 60 days after they were saved.

Revoking access

If you are the owner of a Threads account that has connected to this app, you can remove its access in Threads: Settings, Account, Website permissions.

See also the Privacy Policy.
