Privacy Policy

Spectre Assistant. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

1. What this app is

Spectre Assistant is a private tool used by a single person, the owner of the Threads account @ilya.kelnovich (design studio SPECTRE), to manage that account. It publishes the owner's own posts and helps the owner find public posts in which people ask for website, design, motion or 3D services, so the owner can reply to them. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

2. Data we access

  • The owner's Threads profile and posts (username, account ID, posts the owner publishes), through the owner's own access token.
  • Public Threads posts that match search keywords such as "need a website" (text, author username, link, time). These are posts that their authors made public.

We do not collect private posts, private messages, followers lists, email addresses, phone numbers or any data from Threads users who have not made it public.

3. How we use it

  • To publish the owner's posts on a schedule.
  • To identify public posts where someone is looking for design or development services.
  • To draft a short reply. Every reply is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is published. The app does not send direct messages through the Threads API.

We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising, do not build profiles of people, and do not use it to train machine learning models.

4. Third-party processing

The text of a matching public post is sent to the Claude API provided by Anthropic in order to classify the post and draft a reply. Anthropic processes this text under its own privacy policy. No other third party receives Threads data.

5. Storage and retention

Data is stored in a local database on the owner's own computer. Saved public posts and related records are deleted automatically after 60 days. The access token is stored locally and is never shared.

6. Your choices and deletion

If you believe a post of yours has been saved by this app and want it removed, or if you have any privacy question, see our data deletion instructions or contact us on Telegram: @hovatsavsem. We will delete the data within 7 days. You can also stop the app from accessing your Threads data at any time in Threads: Settings, Account, Website permissions.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.

8. Contact

Telegram: @hovatsavsem, or a direct message to @ilya.kelnovich on Threads.
